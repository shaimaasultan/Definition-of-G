The simulation keeps ε fixed as Earth moves, so the force is F′ = F/ε at every distance. From a tangential start at 1 AU this gives an exact ellipse with eccentricity e = |ε − 1|, semi-major axis a = 1/(2 − ε) AU, and escape when ε ≥ 2. The barycenter split (r₁ ≈ 1 AU, r₂ ≈ 449 km) has ε ≈ 3×10⁻⁶: the force is about 333,000 times too strong, far outside the slider.
