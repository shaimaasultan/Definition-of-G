The measuring point

  1. Any shared point P must sit on the green Cassini oval. It wraps around both bodies.
  2. Equal split (r₁ = r₂ = r): P is the apex of an equilateral triangle on M₁M₂.
  3. On the line the oval crosses at the golden ratio: r₁ = 0.618 r and r₂ = 1.618 r, or the reverse.
  4. Mass rule r₁/r₂ = m₁/m₂ needs r₁ = r√(m₁/m₂) and r₂ = r√(m₂/m₁). Those two circles meet only when 0.382 ≤ m₁/m₂ ≤ 2.618.
