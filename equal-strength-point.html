Equal-Strength Point

Created by Shaimaa Said Soltan

Option C writes Newton's law as F = F_max · (ρ₁/r₁) · (ρ₂/r₂), with gravitational radius ρ = G·m/c² and F_max = c⁴/G. P is where both ratios are equal: each body is equally "strong" there. Drag the purple level on the chart.

Each line is one body's ratio ρ/d. Where a line meets the purple level is that body's P: Earth reaches it at r₁, the Sun at r₂. Solid dots mark each body's surface.
